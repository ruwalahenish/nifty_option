All Members,

Revision in Expiry Day of Index and Stock Derivatives Contracts

This is in partial modification to chapter 1.14 Contract Specification of Index and stock derivatives contracts in
Exchange consolidated circular reference 67775 dated April 30, 2025 and continuation with circular reference 68589
dated June 17,2025 on Revision in Expiry Day of Index and Stock derivatives contracts.

All members are hereby informed to note the following revision in expiry day of the Index and stock derivatives
contracts below:

Derivatives Particulars                  Current Expiry Day          Revised Expiry Day

          NIFTY monthly, quarterly and half Last Thursday of expiry  Last Tuesday of expiry month
                                                                     Tuesday of the week
          yearly contracts               month
                                                                     Last Tuesday of expiry month
          NIFTY weekly contracts         Thursday of the week

Index     BANKNIFTY monthly & quarterly  Last Thursday of expiry
Stocks    contracts                      month

          FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY and
          NIFTYNXT50 monthly contracts

          All Monthly contracts

1. The circular shall come into effect from August 28, 2025 (EOD) i.e. Expiry day for all existing contracts
     will be revised to "New Expiry Day" on August 28, 2025 (EOD).

2. Any new contract created for trading on / after effective date shall be created as per the revised expiry
     day as mentioned in the aforesaid table.

3. Revised expiry date of all existing derivatives contracts shall be available in the contract file which shall be
     generated on August 28,2025 (EOD) end of the day which shall be applicable for trading on August 29,2025.
     To avoid operational complexities, please refer to Annexure for illustration.
