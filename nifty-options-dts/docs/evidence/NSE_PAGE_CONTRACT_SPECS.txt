 Option Type 
 Nifty 50 Index  will have 4 weekly expiration contracts (excluding monthly contracts) and 3 monthly expiration contracts. 
 Nifty Bank Index, Nifty Financial Services Index, Nifty Midcap Select Index, Nifty Next 50 Index and Nifty India FPI 150 Index will have 3 monthly expiration contracts 
 Additionally Nifty 50 Index options and Nifty Bank Index options will have Three quarterly expiries (March, June, Sept & Dec cycle). 
 Nifty 50 index will also have long term index option contracts i.e. next 8 half yearly expiries (Jun, Dec cycle) after the three quarterly expires will be provided. 
 Expiry Day 
 Nifty 50 Index, Nifty Bank Index, Nifty Financial Services Index, Nifty Midcap Select Index, Nifty Next 50 Index and Nifty India FPI 150 Index : Tuesday of the expiry period. If the Tuesday is a trading holiday, then the expiry day is the previous trading day. 
 For Individual Securities: Tuesday of the expiry period. If the last Tuesday is a trading holiday, then the expiry day is the previous trading day.
