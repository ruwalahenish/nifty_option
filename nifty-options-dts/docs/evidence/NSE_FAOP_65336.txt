All Members,

Revision in Expiry Day of Index derivatives contracts

This is in partial modification to Exchange consolidated circular 61814 dated April 29, 2024 chapter 1.14
Contract Specification of Index derivatives contracts.

All members are hereby informed to note the following revision in expiry day of the below Index derivatives
contracts:

Index Derivatives on                     Current Expiry Day              Revised Expiry Day
BANKNIFTY monthly & quarterly contracts  Last Wednesday of expiry month
FINNIFTY monthly contracts               Last Tuesday of expiry month      Last Thursday of
MIDCPNIFTY monthly contracts             Last Monday of expiry month          expiry month
NIFTYNXT50 monthly contracts             Last Friday of expiry month

1. There is no change in expiry day for NIFTY monthly, weekly, quarterly & half yearly contracts.

2. The circular shall come into effect from EOD of January 01, 2025 i.e. Expiry day for all existing
      contracts will be revised to "New Expiry Day" on January 01, 2025 (EOD).

3. Any new contract created for trading on / after effective date shall be created as per the revised expiry
      day as mentioned in the aforesaid table.

4. Revised expiry date of all existing index derivatives contracts shall be available in the contract file
      which shall be generated on January 01, 2025 end of the day which shall be applicable for trading on
      January 02,2025. To avoid operational complexities, please refer to Annexure for illustration.

5. Members are requested to note that there is no other change in the contract specifications of index
      derivatives.
