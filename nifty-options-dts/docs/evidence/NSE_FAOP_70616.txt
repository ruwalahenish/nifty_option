Revision in Market Lot of Derivative Contracts on Indices

In pursuance of SEBI guidelines for periodic revision of lot size for derivatives contracts specified in the SEBI
circular SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, the market lot of derivative
contracts on the following Indices shall be revised as follows:

Sr. No         Underlying Index         Symbol             Present Market Lot  Revised Market lot

   1      Nifty 50                  NIFTY                             75                  65
   2      Nifty Bank                BANKNIFTY                         35                  30
   3      Nifty Financial Services  FINNIFTY                          65                  60
   4      Nifty Mid Select          MIDCPNIFTY                       140                 120

The market lot of derivative contracts on the following Index is unchanged:

Sr. No    Underlying Index              Symbol             Present Market Lot
                                    NIFTYNXT50                        25
